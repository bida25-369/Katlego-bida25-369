About Us

Founded in the heart of Gaborone Botswana, All-round Cars Dealership was built on a simple belief. That every customer deserves a transparent, stress free car buying experience. We offer a diverse selection of quality vehicles to suit every lifestyle and budget. From reliable everyday hatchbacks to premium SUVs, all backed by honest pricing and personalised service. As a proudly local business we are deeply committed to our community and to putting every Motswana behind the wheel of a vehicle they can trust. At All-round Cars Dealership, your journey is our business.
